Casa Clips - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Casa Clips is a tool operated by WorkLess Co. that creates short vertical video clips from authorized live-stream content and publishes them to TikTok accounts owned by the operator.

Information we collect

  • When the account owner connects TikTok through Login Kit, we receive an access token and basic profile information (display name and avatar) of that account.
  • We process video content that the operator already has the right to use. We do not collect personal data from viewers or third parties.

How we use information

  • The access token is used only to upload and publish videos to the connected TikTok account.
  • Profile information is used only to show which account is connected.

Storage and sharing

  • Tokens are stored privately on the operator's own systems and are never shared, sold or disclosed to third parties.
  • Videos are stored temporarily for processing and publishing.

Data retention and deletion

The account owner can revoke access at any time from TikTok settings (Security and permissions > Apps and services). On request, we delete stored tokens and related data.
